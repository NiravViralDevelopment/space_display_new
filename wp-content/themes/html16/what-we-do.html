SINGLE-SOURCE ACCOUNTABILITY

01 / DESIGN & CONCEPT

BOOTH DESIGN & 3D VISUALIZATION

We transform complex brand strategies into immersive 3D spatial environments. Our architectural team creates custom booth concepts, detailed lighting plans, and photorealistic renderings aligned with hall layouts and visitor traffic flows.

  • Custom spatial architecture & brand experience design
  • Photorealistic 3D rendering & virtual walkthroughs
  • Material sampling, finishes & lighting design
02 / ENGINEERING

TECHNICAL PLANNING & PERMITS

Navigating international venue regulations and safety standards. We handle structural static calculations, electrical load distributions, fire retardancy certifications, and official exhibition organizer submissions.

  • Structural engineering & static load calculations
  • Electrical schematics & plumbing submission forms
  • Double-deck & complex hanging rig approvals
03 / FABRICATION

PRODUCTION & CONSTRUCTION

Precision manufacturing across our regional hubs in Poznan, Guangzhou, and Tokyo. We combine traditional Japanese joinery with modern modular aluminum engineering and high-grade laminates.

  • Custom woodwork, millwork & architectural joinery
  • Modular reusable aluminum framing systems
  • Factory pre-assembly & quality control inspection
04 / BRANDING

GRAPHICS & BRAND SIGNAGE

High-definition visual presentation to maximize brand visibility. From seamless backlit fabric lightboxes to 3D acrylic dimensional lettering, vinyl wraps, and overhead hanging banners.

  • Large-format seamless dye-sublimation fabric printing
  • Illuminated tension LED graphics & 3D logos
  • High-durability wall wraps & glass manifestations
05 / TECHNOLOGY

AV & DIGITAL INTEGRATION

Engaging audiences with modern audiovisual technology. We integrate fine-pitch indoor LED walls, interactive touch displays, directional acoustic sound systems, and architectural lighting control.

  • Fine-pitch indoor LED video walls & video controllers
  • Interactive touchscreen kiosks & presentation AV
  • DMX controlled architectural mood & accent lighting
06 / LOGISTICS

LOGISTICS & FREIGHT MANAGEMENT

Eliminating international shipping stress. We coordinate freight consolidation, ATA Carnet customs clearance, local warehouse staging, and direct-to-showfloor delivery.

  • International freight forwarding & customs documentation
  • Venue advance warehouse staging & marshalling
  • Empties storage & post-show return logistics
07 / BUILD

ON-SITE INSTALLATION & DISMANTLE

Professional build crews working under tight venue timelines. Certified carpenters, electricians, and riggers ensure exact adherence to approved blueprints and safety regulations.

  • Certified trade crew supervision & site safety compliance
  • Precision structural erection, painting & detailing
  • Rapid post-event dismantle & eco-friendly recycling
08 / SUPPORT

ON-SITE MANAGEMENT & STANDBY

Continuous support throughout exhibition hours. Dedicated multilingual project managers remain on-site during show days for immediate assistance, daily cleaning, and adjustments.

  • Multilingual project leads present during open hours
  • Immediate technical standby for lighting, AV & graphics
  • Handover documentation & daily maintenance
