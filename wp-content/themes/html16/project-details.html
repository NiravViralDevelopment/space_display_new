PROJECT CASE STUDY

ARCHITECTURAL DESIGN & GLOBAL EXECUTION

Space Display managed the complete end-to-end design, engineering, local fabrication, and on-site build for Wärtsilä’s flagship presentation at Gastech Tokyo.

The booth concept combined Japanese precision timber craft with reusable modular structural systems. Designed to communicate high-efficiency green energy technology, the architecture featured open meeting lounges, high-ceiling suspended tension fabric canopies, and fine-pitch video wall integration.

Our single-point project directors coordinated all venue permits, structural safety certifications, and nightly standby management throughout the event, ensuring flawless execution from initial floor handover to final disassembly.

KEY HIGHLIGHT

Delivered on schedule with 100% venue compliance and zero structural adjustments during show hours.
